# Project Overview

Project Name: Influencer Defense

Description: Influencer Defense is a Sci-Fi Tower Defense game where players strategically deploy "Influencer Towers" to defend against waves of "Bad Ad Bots" attempting to corrupt the internet. Each Influencer Tower represents a different type of influencer or marketing tactic (e.g., Micro-Influencer, Viral Video, Sponsored Post). Players earn resources ("Attention Points") by defeating Bad Ad Bots, which can then be used to upgrade their Influencer Towers, unlock new ones, or deploy special abilities. The "Pages Omni" system inspires the analytics and campaign management aspect, allowing players to track the performance of their towers and optimize their defense strategy. It's a playful way to visualize and understand the dynamics of influencer marketing in a game format.

Features:

*   Tower Defense Gameplay: Core tower defense mechanics with waves of enemies and strategic tower placement.
*   Influencer Towers: Unique towers representing different influencer types and marketing strategies, each with its strengths and weaknesses.
*   Analytics Dashboard: Inspired by Pages Omni, providing data on tower performance, enemy types, and overall campaign effectiveness.
*   Campaign Management: Allow players to plan out their 'marketing campaign' using preset strategies and formations.
*   Sci-Fi Theme: A futuristic setting with visually appealing tower and enemy designs.
